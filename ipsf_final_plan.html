The drive down had a rough patch — a real fever scare, sitting in a hot car, and dehydration that got worse because there was no way to stop for a bathroom for a long stretch. By the time a stop finally happened, it was a genuinely painful hold. All of it got handled once the car stopped, but it was a real dip, not a minor discomfort.

07
Position

CDM Focus

Central defensive midfielder — the four habits that matter most, plus the idea that ties them together.

A
Already a Strength

Scanning

Keep checking shoulders before every touch — this is already working, just keep it up under final-game pressure.

B
New Focus

Open Hips / Body Orientation

Receive side-on before the ball arrives so the first touch can go forward instead of needing an extra step to turn.

C
Under Pressure

First Touch

Push the ball away from pressure and into space — never straight back into the defender closing you down.

D
Off the Ball

Cover Shadow

Press the ball carrier while your body blocks the passing lane behind you — one action, two jobs.

★
Ties It All Together

Decide Before the Ball Arrives

Know your next move while the ball is still traveling to you — not after you receive it. This is what scanning, open hips, and first touch are all in service of.

08
Without the Ball

Defending as a CDM

The goal isn't more tackles — it's stopping anyone from driving through the middle.

1
Don't dive in — stay patient and let the attacker make the mistake.
2
Stay goal-side, back-pedal instead of turning your back on the play.
3
Show the attacker one direction — usually wide, away from the middle.
4
Delay until a teammate arrives to help — you don't have to win it alone.
Drill

1v1 Contain, No Tackling

With a teammate: just delay for 5 seconds, staying on your feet the whole time. No tackling allowed — the only job is containing.

Getting the Ball

Be the Easy Option

Teammates pass to players who look available. Show for the ball in an open body position — don't hide in a passing shadow and then wonder why it never comes.
